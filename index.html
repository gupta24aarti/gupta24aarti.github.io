01 — About

A bit about me

I'm Aarti Gupta, an aspiring Data Scientist on a mission to make data useful. I enjoy the full journey — from cleaning messy datasets and running exploratory analysis, to building machine learning models and presenting findings through clear, interactive dashboards.

My hands-on work spans machine learning (heart-attack risk prediction, customer segmentation), data analysis (Blinkit sales, telecom churn, HR attrition), and SQL & BI (eCommerce analytics, Power BI and Tableau dashboards). I'm always learning and excited to grow into a Data Science role.
